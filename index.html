How does GPS
know where you are?

GPS uses satellites in space to help your phone figure out its location.

★
★

STEP BY STEP

It's actually pretty clever.

Your phone doesn't ask a satellite, “Where am I?”

Instead, it uses signals from several satellites to work it out.

01

Satellites are always up there

GPS satellites orbit Earth and constantly send signals toward the ground.

★
A satellite sends a signal toward Earth.
02

Your phone receives the signal

The signal travels from the satellite to your phone.

Because we know how fast the signal travels, your phone can figure out roughly how far away the satellite is.

★
📱
“The satellite is about this far away.”
03

One satellite isn't enough

Imagine you know you're exactly 20 km away from a satellite.

You could be anywhere on a huge circle around that satellite.

★
📱
One satellite gives your phone a distance, but not your exact location.
04

More satellites make it clearer

Your phone does the same thing with several satellites.

The possible locations get smaller and smaller until the phone can find where the distances meet.

★
★
★
YOU
Where the measurements meet is your location.
05

And that's how your phone finds you

Your phone combines the information from several satellites and calculates your position.

●
Location found

Your phone now knows where you are.
