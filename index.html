Want the nerdy version?

GPS is basically
very precise timing.

Your phone receives radio signals from GPS satellites. Because those signals travel at a known speed, the phone can use the tiny difference between the sending and receiving times to estimate how far away each satellite is.

In the real world, GPS works in three dimensions. Your phone normally uses signals from at least four satellites to calculate its position and correct for the phone's own clock error.

Radio signals Satellites continuously broadcast information that your receiver can understand.
Travel time The receiver measures how long the signal took to arrive.
Trilateration Several distance measurements are combined to calculate the position.
